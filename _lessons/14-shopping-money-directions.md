Recall prepositions of place from [[07-house-home]] — **in, op, onder, naast, achter, voor, tussen** — they're used constantly for directions too:

| Dutch | Pronunciation | English | Persian |

## Summary

- Shopping phrases center on **kosten** (to cost), **betalen** (to pay), and **Hoeveel kost...?**
- Directions rely on **rechtdoor/links/rechts** plus the place-prepositions from Lesson 07.
- **Kunt u me helpen?** is your universal polite opener for asking anyone for help.
