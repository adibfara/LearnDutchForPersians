# How to use this course
### <span dir="rtl" lang="fa">چگونه از این دوره استفاده کنیم</span>

This course has **16 lessons** (00–15), numbered in the order you should study them. Each one builds on the vocabulary and grammar of the ones before it, so it's best not to skip around on a first pass.

<span dir="rtl" lang="fa">این دوره ۱۶ درس دارد (۰۰ تا ۱۵) که به ترتیبی که باید خوانده شوند شماره‌گذاری شده‌اند. هر درس بر پایه واژگان و گرامر درس‌های قبلی ساخته می‌شود، پس بهتر است در دور اول آن‌ها را به ترتیب بخوانید.</span>

## Every lesson has the same shape

1. **Objectives** — what you'll be able to say/understand by the end
2. **Vocabulary table** — Dutch, a simple pronunciation guide, English, and Persian
3. **Grammar** — explained in English, with Persian notes for the tricky bits (especially where Dutch and Persian grammar work very differently)
4. **Examples / dialogue** — real sentences using the new words
5. **Practice quiz** — click an answer, get instant feedback, see your score
6. **Summary** — a quick recap box at the end

## Tips for studying / <span dir="rtl" lang="fa">نکاتی برای مطالعه</span>

- Say the Dutch words out loud — pronunciation matters more than spelling in the beginning.
- <span dir="rtl" lang="fa">کلمات هلندی را بلند تلفظ کنید — در ابتدا تلفظ مهم‌تر از املا است.</span>
- Don't just read the vocabulary table — cover the English/Persian columns and try to recall the Dutch word.
- Retake each quiz after a day or two — spaced repetition beats cramming.
- Levels: **A0** lessons assume zero prior Dutch; **A1** lessons assume you've completed the A0 lessons before them.

## Level guide

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A0 | Absolute beginner — sounds, first words, first phrases |
| A1 | Basic user — simple everyday exchanges, present-tense sentences |
